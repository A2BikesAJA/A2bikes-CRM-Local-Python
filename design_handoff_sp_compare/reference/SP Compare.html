Hand-laid Toray T700 carbon — the same material used in pro tri bikes costing $10K+. Designed by Kevin Quan, the engineer behind the Cervélo P-Series.

SAGS adjustable cockpit

The most adjustable triathlon cockpit available — 140mm of fore/aft range fits riders short to tall without giving up aero position. Free size exchanges if you need to swap.

Kevin Quan designed both. The SP is the direct-to-consumer evolution of that design philosophy at a lower price point. Cervélo has more brand prestige and a dealer network; we have direct support and free crash replacement. For most athletes, the SP delivers the same race-day performance at a fraction of the cost. We offer free size exchanges with no time limit on the offer — if the frame size isn't right, we'll get you the correct one. Between the sizing tool, the quiz, and the SAGS cockpit's 140mm of adjustability, most riders dial in their fit on the first try.

The bike arrives 92% assembled. You'll install the seatpost, attach the cockpit, and adjust the brakes — the headset and stem are the fiddly parts, so take your time with the torque wrench in the box. Most riders finish in under an hour with the included video guide. If you'd rather skip the work, most local bike shops will do the final assembly for $50–150.

At checkout we place a temporary authorization hold on your card — we don't charge you. Your demo starts when the bike arrives. Ride it for a week, and if it's not right, send it back and pay nothing. You're only charged at the end of the demo period if you keep it.
